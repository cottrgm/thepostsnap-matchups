▶

Where the numbers come from

Raw play-by-play, box scores, ratings, betting lines and weather from CollegeFootballData.com — every FBS snap, both sides of the ball, re-pulled and re-parsed twice a day, at 5:00am and 4:00pm Central.

The statistics are computed from the feeds, never typed in. The exceptions are injury and roster news: a major injury, dismissal or transfer is sometimes added by hand from a linked news report before the feeds catch up.

Also used: PFF snap counts and grades, 247Sports recruiting ratings, RotoWire's injury wire (conference availability reports) and venue data from CollegeFootballData.
